## About me

I am a software engineer and researcher with a PhD in computer science from PUCRS (Brazil) and nearly eight years of experience spanning industry, research and development (R&D) projects, and academia.

My interests include algorithms, machine learning, and optimization, with a particular emphasis on resource management in parallel and distributed systems.

In addition to my research background, I have strong hands-on experience in software and data engineering, using technologies such as Kubernetes, Docker, pandas, PySpark, cloud platforms, and database systems.
